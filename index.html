Speed Advantage

Sees the load before the page does

Amazon sends load data from its servers to your browser. DOM-scraping tools wait for the page to fully render before they can read a single number. Prohauling Relay overrides the browser's native fetch() and XMLHttpRequest APIs to intercept the raw JSON payload the moment the server response arrives — before the rendering pipeline even starts.

Full Feature Set

Everything a serious carrier needs

Built by carriers, for carriers. No fluff — just the tools that put money in your pocket.

⚡
4-Step Auto-Booking Engine
When a qualifying load is detected, the engine: (1) locates the load card in the DOM, (2) opens the detail view, (3) waits out your configurable countdown window, then (4) confirms the booking — all without you touching anything. Fast Book mode also auto-dismisses Amazon's secondary confirmation dialog, cutting booking time to the absolute minimum.
🎯
9-Dimension Real-Time Filtering
Every incoming load is evaluated against 9 filter criteria simultaneously: minimum total payout, minimum $/mile, maximum distance, origin states, destination states, equipment type, platform AI score (0–100), minimum pay/hour, and duplicate suppression. A load must pass every active filter before any action is taken.
📲
5-Channel Alert System
All five channels fire simultaneously on a qualifying load: audio alert (custom sound library), voice synthesis (speaks payout, route, rate out loud), Telegram message to your phone, visual overlay banner on the load board, and flash animation on the load card. Never miss a load whether you're watching the screen or not.
🔈
Voice Announcements
The extension uses the browser's built-in speech synthesis API to speak load details aloud — "Two thousand eight hundred forty dollars, Chicago to Dallas, five eighty-two per mile." You hear the load without looking at the screen — hands on the wheel, ears on the board.
📅
Schedule & Shift Hours
Define up to 5 time windows per week. Auto-booking activates only during your configured shift hours. Outside your schedule, the monitor still watches and alerts — it just won't book without you.
👥
Fleet & Multi-Driver Management
Running a small fleet? Create independent driver profiles, each with its own filter set, Telegram target, alert preferences, and schedule. Switch between drivers in one click.
🔌
Network-Level API Interception
By overriding the browser's native fetch() and XMLHttpRequest APIs, Prohauling Relay receives load data the instant the server response arrives — before rendering starts. Monitors all load board endpoints: /tours/loadboard, /loadboard/search, /loadboard/recommendations. Layout changes on Amazon's front-end have no effect.
🌟
25 Themes & Spanish Support
Choose from 25 color themes to match your style. Full English and Spanish language support throughout the interface — for drivers and dispatchers who work in both.
Get Running in Minutes

Three steps to hands-free hauling

1
Install & Configure
Add Prohauling Relay to Chrome. Enter your license key, set your minimum payout, preferred lanes, equipment type, and the schedule windows you want to work. Takes about five minutes.
2
Extension Monitors 24/7
Open the Amazon Relay load board and leave it running. Prohauling Relay intercepts every API response in the background — scanning new loads the instant they appear, around the clock.
3
Best Loads Get Booked
A qualifying load triggers all 5 alert channels at once and starts the auto-book countdown. All 4 booking steps execute automatically. If you want to cancel, you have the window. If you don't — the load is yours. Your phone rings. You drive.
