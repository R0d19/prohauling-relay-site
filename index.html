Auto-Booking Setup

Set it once. Let it work for you.

Auto-booking is off by default. You turn it on when you're ready. Here's how it works once you do.

⚙
Step 1 — Tell it what you want
Open the extension and set your minimums: the lowest payout you'll accept, your minimum dollars per mile, the states you want to run from and to, and your equipment type. You can be as specific or as open as you want.
⏱
Step 2 — Set your countdown window
Choose how many seconds the extension waits before booking — anywhere from 3 to 30 seconds. When a good load shows up, a countdown starts on your screen. If you want to cancel, just click. If you don't — it books for you.
📅
Step 3 — Set your schedule (optional)
Only want it to book loads on weekday mornings? Set your work windows. Auto-booking only runs during those hours. Outside your schedule, it still alerts you — it just won't book without you there.
⚡
Step 4 — Turn it on and walk away
Flip the auto-booking switch, leave the load board open in Chrome, and you're done. The extension watches the board. When a load matches every filter you set, it opens it, waits for your countdown, and books it — no clicks needed.
📲
Step 5 — Set up your Telegram & phone alerts
Every qualifying load triggers all your alerts at once. Here's how to set them up:

Telegram: Open Telegram, search for @GetMyid_Bot and send it any message — it replies with your numeric Chat ID. Paste that ID into the extension settings. From that point on, every matching load sends you a message like this:
🚛 QUALIFYING LOAD
💰 Payout: $2,840
📍 Route: Chicago, IL → Dallas, TX
📏 Distance: 487 mi
💵 Rate: $5.82/mi
🏷 Equipment: Dry Van
Phone call: Enter your driver's phone number in the Driver settings. When a load matches, the extension places an automated call that speaks the load details out loud — payout, route, and distance.

This is especially useful during late-night shifts. If a driver is monitoring loads but gets drowsy, the phone call acts as an alarm — it rings even when the screen is locked or the driver stepped away. A load worth $3,000 at 2 AM won't go unnoticed.
🔒
You're always in control
Auto-booking will never book a load that doesn't match your filters. The countdown window gives you time to cancel any booking before it goes through. You can turn it off with one click at any time.
