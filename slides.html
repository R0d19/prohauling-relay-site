Slide 06 — Auto Book
Auto Book Engine
Set your criteria once — Prohauling books matching loads for you, even while you're away from the screen.
1
Set Your Minimums
In Settings, enter your minimum rate per mile (e.g. $4.00/mi) and minimum total pay. Only loads above both thresholds qualify.
2
Enable Auto Book
Toggle on "Auto Book Enabled." A separate booking timer runs independently from your scan — set it 1–30 seconds.
⏱ Two independent timers: scan timer + booking timer
3
Prohauling Books It
When a qualifying load appears, Prohauling clicks the Book button automatically. The card gets an AUTO BOOK badge.
4
You Get Notified
A sound plays and — if configured — a Telegram message or voice call confirms the booking with full load details.
How the two timers work

Refreshes load list

Slide 07 — Deadhead
Deadhead Time Check
Prohauling calculates how long it actually takes you to drive empty to the pickup — and warns you before you commit.
How Deadhead Time is Calculated
DH Time = (Deadhead Miles ÷ Avg Speed) × 60 × 1.10
The ×1.10 adds a 10% buffer for traffic, stops, and real-world delays.

Pro Tip
Set your Average Speed to match what you realistically drive — depending on the traffic in your area. City drivers → 35–40 mph. Highway runners → 55–65 mph. Each load can also be toggled individually.
Slide 08 — Filters & Safety
Smart Filters & Ban Protection
Narrow down the loads you see and keep your account safe from automated bot detection.
💵
Rate per Mile
Hide loads below your minimum $/mi threshold. Only loads worth your time appear.
📦
Total Pay
Filter by minimum total payout per load, regardless of distance.
📏
Min / Max Miles
Set a mileage range. Skip short local hauls or very long overnight runs.
🕐
Pickup Window
Only show loads with pickups within a time range that fits your schedule.
📍
Origin Zone
Filter loads by pickup city or region. Focus on your home area.
🚫
Block Destinations
Never want to go to certain cities? Add them to your block list.
🛡️
BAN RISK Protection
The platform monitors for bot-like behavior — refreshing too fast is a red flag. When your scan interval is set too aggressively (under ~3–4 seconds), the ⚠ BAN RISK badge appears in the bar. Increase your scan interval to make it disappear. Prohauling adds small random jitter to request timing to look more human, but staying in a safe range is your best protection.
Slide 09 — Quick Start
Get Running in 5 Steps
New to Prohauling? Here's all you need to start finding and booking loads right now.
1
🌐
Go to the Load Board
Open the load board and log in. The Prohauling bar will appear at the top automatically.
2
⚙️
Open Settings
Click ⚙ SETTINGS and set your minimum rate, minimum pay, and scan interval.
3
🔔
Enable Alerts
Turn on sound alerts. Optionally add your Telegram bot token or phone number for remote alerts.
4
▶️
Press START
Hit the teal START button. The bar turns green and scanning begins. You'll hear a horn on new loads.
5
⚡
Enable Auto Book
Set your auto-book minimums in Settings and turn it on. Sit back — Prohauling handles bookings for you.
